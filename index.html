TCS iON / Digialm Answer Key Logic

How the Scanner Evaluates & Crops Your Answer Key PDF or Link

Modern competitive exam response sheets (TCS iON / RRB / SSC / Digialm) highlight the Official Correct Option in Green with a Checkmark (✔) (numbered 1, 2, 3, 4 or A, B, C, D) and wrong options in Red with a Cross (✗) on the left, while showing your Chosen Option in the metadata box on the right.

Case 1: Correct Match ✓ CORRECT (+1.00)

• Official Green Tick: ✔ Option 3 (or D)
• Candidate's Chosen Option: Chosen Option : 3 (or D)
• Result: Since Chosen Option matches the Green Tick option, full positive marks are awarded.

Case 2: Mismatch ✗ WRONG (-0.33)

• Official Green Tick: ✔ Option 1 (or A)
• Candidate's Chosen Option: Chosen Option : 3 (or B)
• Result: Since Chosen Option differs from the Green Tick option, negative marks are deducted.

Case 3: Unattempted — SKIPPED (0.00)

• Candidate's Chosen Option: Chosen Option : --
• Status: Not Answered or Not Attempted and Marked For Review
• Result: Marked as Unattempted with zero marks impact.
