TCS iON / Digialm Answer Key Logic

How the Scanner Evaluates & Crops Your Answer Key PDF

Modern competitive exam response sheets (TCS iON / Digialm) highlight the Official Correct Option in Green with a Checkmark (✔) and wrong options in Red with a Cross (✗) on the left, while showing your Chosen Option in the metadata box on the right.

Example 1: Question Q.3 (ID: 4410091451941) ✗ WRONG (-Negative)

• Official Correct Answer: Option A (Highlighted in green with ✔ A. আদিমতা)
• Candidate's Chosen Option: Option B (Shown in right table: Chosen Option : B)
• Evaluation: Since Chosen (B) ≠ Correct (A), Q.3 is marked Wrong and deducts negative marks.

Example 2: Question Q.4 (ID: 4410091451825) ✓ CORRECT (+Positive)

• Official Correct Answer: Option D (Highlighted in green with ✔ D. গুচ্ছ - অর্থে)
• Candidate's Chosen Option: Option D (Shown in right table: Chosen Option : D)
• Evaluation: Since Chosen (D) = Correct (D), Q.4 is marked Correct and awards positive marks.

Unattempted Questions: When Chosen Option : -- or Status : Not Answered, the question is marked as Unattempted (Skipped) with 0.00 marks impact.
